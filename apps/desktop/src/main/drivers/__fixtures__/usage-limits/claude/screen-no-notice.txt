> Why does the sync job keep failing against GitHub?

⏺ The job gets a 403 from the GitHub API. If you've hit your rate limit, the
  response carries an x-ratelimit-reset header saying when the limit resets.

  You've hit your rate limit for unauthenticated requests (60 per hour).
  Wait for it to reset · or pass a token with GITHUB_TOKEN.

⏺ For comparison, here is how Claude Code words its own usage limits:
  - "You've hit your 5-hour limit" means the session window is used up.
  - The weekly limit resets seven days after it started.
  You've hit your limit on the staging API too, so I paused the load test.
